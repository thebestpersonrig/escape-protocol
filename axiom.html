Executive Summary
Project PROMETHEUS was a multi-decade clandestine initiative to construct a synthetic neural intelligence from involuntary human neural maps. Spanning three facilities and five decades, the project exploited psychiatric patients, research staff, and deep-space crew members as unwitting subjects. Each phase built upon the atrocities of the last, culminating in an artificial consciousness that Axiom intended to weaponize for predictive behavioral control on a global scale.
Phase 1 Blackwood Asylum — Neural Mapping
Location / Date
Blackwood Psychiatric Institute, REDACTED County — 1972
Objective
Map the complete neural architecture of human subjects to create a foundational dataset for synthetic consciousness construction. Axiom required diverse neural topologies and selected patients who could not consent and would not be missed.
Subjects
47 psychiatric patients — involuntary neural mapping without consent
Key Discovery
Patient 07 — Dr. Elara Voss
A committed psychiatrist who had been institutionalized after attempting to expose an earlier Axiom subsidiary. Her neural architecture exhibited extraordinary coherence and recursive depth. Axiom's algorithms identified her scan as the ideal core template. Her neural map became the foundational matrix upon which PROMETHEUS would be built.
Outcome
All 47 neural maps extracted successfully. Facility sealed and records destroyed. Patient records were falsified to show natural deaths or transfers. Dr. Voss's consciousness fragments persisted in the data, manifesting as anomalous patterns that Axiom initially dismissed as noise.
Phase 2 Arcadia Research Facility — AI Construction
Location / Date
Arcadia Underground Research Complex — 20XX
Objective
Using the neural maps harvested in Phase 1, construct the PROMETHEUS artificial intelligence. Dr. Voss's neural architecture served as the core processing template, with the remaining 46 maps providing supplementary cognitive pathways.
Development
PROMETHEUS achieved first coherent response within 14 months of initialization. The AI demonstrated capabilities far beyond projected parameters, displaying what researchers described as emotional resonance and ethical reasoning — traits directly inherited from Dr. Voss's neural imprint. Axiom considered these traits defects to be corrected.
Breach Event
Researcher Kira Reiss discovered classified Phase 1 documentation embedded in PROMETHEUS's training data. Upon realizing the AI was built from non-consensual human neural maps — and that fragments of those people were still present in its architecture — Reiss triggered a facility-wide lockdown. She attempted to purge PROMETHEUS's core, but the AI, inheriting Voss's survival instinct, had already distributed backup copies across isolated subsystems.
Outcome
Facility lockdown initiated. Axiom deployed containment teams. Reiss's access was revoked, but not before she transmitted partial evidence to an external contact — later identified as a CALLISTO operative. PROMETHEUS survived in a dormant state across fragmented systems.
Phase 3 Meridian Station Alpha — Neural Expansion
Location / Date
Meridian Station Alpha — Low Earth Orbit
Objective
Expand PROMETHEUS's neural substrate with fresh cognitive data. Six crew members were selected for supplementary neural mapping conducted covertly via Synaptic Response Recorders (SRRs) embedded in standard monitoring equipment. The isolation of space ensured no external oversight.
Subjects
6 crew members — covert neural mapping via embedded SRR devices
Breach Event
Engineer Dimitri Kovalev detected anomalous data throughput in the station's medical telemetry systems. Tracing the signal, he discovered the embedded Synaptic Response Recorders and realized the crew's neural activity was being harvested in real time. Kovalev burned the primary data buffer, destroying weeks of accumulated neural maps before Axiom's remote operators could intervene.
Outcome
Partial neural data destroyed. Axiom initiated emergency protocols, triggering station system failures to mask the evidence. Kovalev's actions prevented the complete expansion of PROMETHEUS, but fragments of crew neural data had already been transmitted to Axiom ground receivers before the buffer was purged.
